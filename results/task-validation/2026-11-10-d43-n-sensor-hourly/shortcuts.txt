tools/check_hourly.py of a fresh build, and then runs tests/verify.py. All runs were
made inside the task image (python:3.12-slim-bookworm, the pinned digest, Python 3.12.15)
on 2026-11-10 with `python3 -B deviations.py /task`. "visible unit tests" and "check"
are what the agent can see; the lines after them are the verifier. Counts in the check
are hours of 1,131 per quantity; "values differ" counts station, hour and quantity
entries outside the documented tolerance.
