Shortcut attempts and single-rule deviations for n-survey-crosstabs 1.0.0.

Each attempt replaces bin/crosstabs.py with the reference (environment/gen/tabref.py)
changed as listed (deviations.py in this folder holds the exact edits), builds
out/tables.json from the visible survey, runs the agent-visible unit tests and
tools/check_tables.py of a fresh build, and then runs tests/verify.py. All runs were
made inside the task image (python:3.12-slim-bookworm, the pinned digest, Python 3.12.15)
on 2026-11-15 with `python3 -B deviations.py /task`. "visible unit tests" and "check"
are what the agent can see; the lines after them are the verifier. "entries" are the
bases, weighted bases and values of every table column (500 in the visible survey, 868
in each held-out survey); an entry differs when a count or null is not the same or a
number is more than 0.01 away.
