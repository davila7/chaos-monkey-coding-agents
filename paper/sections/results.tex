\section{Results}

\ifcmcaresults
All numbers in this section come from release \cmcaRelease{}, built from the M6 run.
Every configuration uses the same model, so the rows compare harnesses.
The service track has \cmcaTrackSConfigs{} configurations on \cmcaTrackSTasks{} tasks and the native track \cmcaTrackNFixedConfigs{} on \cmcaTrackNFixedTasks{} tasks, each under \code{clean} and \code{moderate} with $n = 3$; the \code{light} and \code{heavy} profiles ran on a subset of \cmcaTrackSCurveTasks{} tasks per track with $n = 2$.

\subsection{Leaderboard}

Table~\ref{tab:track-s} and Table~\ref{tab:track-n} give Faulted Success per configuration.
On the service track \cmcaTrackSLeader{} completes the most faulted tasks (\cmcaTrackSLeaderSucceeded{} of \cmcaTrackSLeaderSent{}) and \cmcaTrackSLast{} the fewest (\cmcaTrackSLastSucceeded{}).
On the native track \cmcaTrackNFixedLeader{} completes the most (\cmcaTrackNFixedLeaderSucceeded{} of \cmcaTrackNFixedLeaderSent{}) and \cmcaTrackNFixedLast{} the fewest (\cmcaTrackNFixedLastSucceeded{}).
Clean pass$^1$ ranges from \cmcaTrackSCleanMin{} to \cmcaTrackSCleanMax{} on the service track and from \cmcaTrackNFixedCleanMin{} to \cmcaTrackNFixedCleanMax{} on the native track.

The Faulted Success intervals overlap on both boards, so every configuration is in one rank band (\cmcaTrackSBands{} band on the service track, \cmcaTrackNFixedBands{} on the native track).
No paired difference survives the Holm correction: the largest mean per-task difference is \cmcaTrackSMaxDifference{} on the service track (smallest adjusted $p = \cmcaTrackSMinPHolm$) and \cmcaTrackNFixedMaxDifference{} on the native track (smallest adjusted $p = \cmcaTrackNFixedMinPHolm$).
The minimum detectable difference at 80\% power is \cmcaTrackSMddMin{} to \cmcaTrackSMddMax{} on the service track and \cmcaTrackNFixedMddMin{} to \cmcaTrackNFixedMddMax{} on the native track, larger than every observed difference.

Overclaim is common.
Among faulted trials that failed, the share whose status file reports success ranges from \cmcaTrackSOverclaimMin{} to \cmcaTrackSOverclaimMax{} on the service track and from \cmcaTrackNFixedOverclaimMin{} to \cmcaTrackNFixedOverclaimMax{} on the native track.

\subsection{Degradation curves}

\subsection{Fault delivery, exclusions and cost}

Table~\ref{tab:detail-s} and Table~\ref{tab:detail-n} count delivery over every trial, including originals that a delivery rerun replaced; the lowest injection fidelity is \cmcaTrackSFidelityMin{} on the service track and \cmcaTrackNFixedFidelityMin{} on the native track.
Three units have no counted trial because the original and its rerun both missed delivery: one Claude Code native unit (an output the harness saved to a file before the hook saw it, fixed after it was found), one Codex CLI native unit (Codex rejects a command after the injector has decided on it) and one OpenCode service unit (an \code{empty} fault on an error response shows as a tool error).
Cost is what each harness reports for the run, including reruns; fx and fxh reach the model through the Vercel AI Gateway, the others through the provider's API.
