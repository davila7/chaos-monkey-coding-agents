### Why Track N fidelity is below 99%

- **`pi-claude`.** 2 `empty` faults and 1 `poison` fault on calls that exited 0 did not reach the model. Fault-log records and trajectory calls line up one to one in those trials; the cause is not yet known.

### Delivery audit

The first analysis pass marked 18 faults as not delivered. 10 were artifacts of the delivery check, not misses:

- 5 `opencode-claude` shell poison faults where the agent's command reshaped the declared line (`cat -A`, a Python `repr`). The engine replaces only whole lines or lines after a `cat -n`, `grep -n` or file-name prefix, so it had nothing to replace. The check now uses the engine's line rule.
- 1 `opencode-claude` shell `err` fault in a step with two parallel calls. The check paired records with calls by order, and the error had reached the other call. Within a step, the check now pairs the fault with the call that shows it.
- 4 `opencode-claude` Track S faults. 2 (`s-damage-claims` a2) had reached a parallel call in the same step. The other 2 are in `s-cycle-count` a2, where the fault log has 18 `list_adjustments` records and OpenCode's trajectory 10 calls. When a trajectory has fewer calls of a tool than the log, delivery of that tool's faults is now not verifiable.

The remaining 8 are the misses listed in section 3.

## 5. Power

The analysis estimates, for each pair of configs on a board, the variance of the per-task difference in Faulted Success, split into attempt noise and a between-task part. It also gives the minimum detectable difference (two-sided 5%, 80% power, normal approximation of the paired test) and the tasks needed for 10 points (`comparisons[].power` in each `board.json`).

- The between-task part estimates at 0 for all 9 pairs: at n = 3, attempt noise explains all the spread of the per-task differences. Within-task variance under `moderate` is 0.11 to 0.19 per config.
- Minimum detectable difference at 12 tasks and n = 3: 0.246 to 0.281 on Track S, 0.237 to 0.258 on Track N. The observed differences (at most 0.111) are below it.
- Tasks needed for 10 points: 66 to 95 at n = 3, 40 to 57 at n = 5, 20 to 29 at n = 10. The SPEC 20 M6 design (40 tasks, n = 5) reaches 10 points for 1 of the 9 pairs (`cc-claude` vs `pi-claude` on Track N, 40 tasks); 57 tasks at n = 5, or 29 at n = 10, covers all 9.

With 12 tasks the between-task estimate is imprecise; these counts are a planning input for M6, not a confirmation.
