D-0052 stage 1b, 2026-11-11, in the cloud container. M6 data under the pre-registration tag `pre-m6-2026-11-10` (commit d36bdb6c). Evidence: `results/pilot/2026-11-11-m6-s/` (commands in `commands.txt`, per-trial data in `summary.json`). Boards are not built from this stage alone; they come from one release after stage 4 (D-0052 consequences). The success counts below are raw counts for the stage check, not board metrics.

## 1. What ran

18 Track S tasks (METHODOLOGY 2.5) x 5 configs x `clean` and `moderate` x 3 attempts = 540 trials, seed `m6-2026-11-10`.

- Infrastructure failures: 2 of 540 (0.4%), both `npm install` of Claude Code at agent setup. Each was rerun once and passed.
- The container restarted once during attempt 0 `clean`. 9 trials of `cc-claude`, `codex-claude` and `opencode-claude` were cut and finished with `harbor jobs resume`. Their model spend before the cut is in no `result.json` and is not counted below.
