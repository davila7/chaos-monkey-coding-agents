# M6 stage 4: curve profiles `light` and `heavy`

D-0052 stage 4, 2026-11-17 18:58 UTC to 2026-11-18 06:33 UTC, in the cloud container, from `main` at 457c8c3b (the curve subset, commit 2de08477, recorded in D-0052 Progress before any stage 4 trial). Evidence: `results/pilot/2026-11-18-m6-s4-s/` (Track S) and `results/pilot/2026-11-18-m6-s4-n/` (Track N), each with `commands.txt` and `summary.json`. The success counts below are raw counts for the stage check, not board metrics.

## 1. What ran

The 20-task curve subset per track, profiles `light` and `heavy`, attempts 0 and 1, seed `m6-2026-11-10`, the ten configs: 80 trials per config and track, 800 in total. Track S ran first, then Track N. The runner started a job only with at least 15 GB of free disk, while the measured stage 4 Anthropic spend was under $145, and while the Vercel balance was at least $12. Attempt 1 of `fx-claude` and `fxh-claude` waited for the owner's Vercel top-up ($20, 2026-11-17 19:57 UTC).

- No archived trial is an infrastructure failure (every one has a verifier reward).
- 10 infrastructure failures, none with a model cost, were rerun before archiving: 8 `n-wheel-build` trials whose task image build got `429 Too Many Requests` from Docker Hub, one `cc-claude` agent install that failed with `npm ERR! code ECONNRESET`, and one `pi-claude` agent setup timeout. The failed trial directories are in `jobs/m6/stage4/_infra/`.
- 9 delivery reruns (METHODOLOGY 6), section 3. The tables count each rerun in place of its original; the cost totals include both.

The container restarted three times (2026-11-18 about 00:36, 03:28 and 04:40 UTC). Each restart stopped `dockerd`, the runner and every trial in flight. 8 Track N trials were cut: 2 `cc-claude` and 6 `codex-claude`. Their directories were moved to `jobs/m6/stage4/_cut/` (not archived) and their jobs resumed with `harbor jobs resume`, which ran the missing trials from scratch. The spend of the cut trials is in no `result.json` and is not measured. The cause of the restarts is not known from inside the container; the kernel version changed after the first one.

While recovering, the runner's skip check turned out to accept a `result.json` whose `finished_at` is null, which would have skipped the two jobs the first restart cut. The continuation runner checks for a non-null `finished_at` and resumes unfinished jobs.
